# 01 Research Papers Archive

This directory stores key academic literature and benchmark papers reviewed for the Big Data Security and Privacy research project.

## Index of Reviewed Papers

### 1. Sweeney (2002) - k-Anonymity
- **Title:** k-Anonymity: A Model for Protecting Privacy
- **Domain:** Privacy-Preserving Data Publishing
- **Contribution:** Introduced quasi-identifier generalization and suppression to prevent identity disclosure in published data.

### 2. Machanavajjhala et al. (2007) - l-Diversity
- **Title:** l-Diversity: Privacy Beyond k-Anonymity
- **Domain:** Attribute Disclosure Protection
- **Contribution:** Resolved background knowledge attack vulnerabilities inherent in k-anonymity by requiring attribute diversity within equivalence classes.

### 3. Li et al. (2007) - t-Closeness
- **Title:** t-Closeness: Privacy Beyond k-Anonymity and l-Diversity
- **Domain:** Data Distribution Privacy
- **Contribution:** Used Earth Mover's Distance to restrict sensitive attribute distribution divergence within anonymized groups.

### 4. Dwork (2008) - Differential Privacy
- **Title:** Differential Privacy: A Survey of Results
- **Domain:** Mathematical Privacy Models
- **Contribution:** Established formal mathematical privacy guarantees through Laplace and Gaussian noise perturbation.

### 5. Erlingsson et al. (2014) - RAPPOR
- **Title:** RAPPOR: Randomized Aggregatable Privacy-Preserving Ordinal Response
- **Domain:** Local Differential Privacy (LDP)
- **Contribution:** Enabled client-side noise injection for privacy-preserving crowdsourced telemetry collection.

### 6. McMahan et al. (2017) - Federated Learning
- **Title:** Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data
- **Domain:** Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning
- **Contribution:** Combined distributed machine learning with differential privacy to train models without centralizing raw data.
